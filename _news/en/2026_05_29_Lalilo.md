We presented the preliminary results of our RCT evaluating the effectiveness of Lalilo, an edtech aiming to improvement first grade students reading skills, during the conference celebrating the achievements of the IDEE program. You can a summary of the results on the [IDEE website](https://www.idee-education.fr/projet/lalilo/) as well as an accompanying [blog post by Lalilo](https://blog.lalilo.com/etude-dimpact-lalilo-premiers-resultats/). Our next steps: explain in greater depth the use of Lalilo, and explore heterogeneous effects.
